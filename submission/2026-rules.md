## What changed from the 2023 sheet optimisation.md was written from

| | 2023 sheet | 2026 guide |
|---|---|---|
| Scale | not printed; half points | "evaluated on a scale of 1-to-5, in each of the following criteria" |
| Validity | scientifically valid, does what it says, real world | adds: "The completeness of the proposed solution varies … Additionally, the **usability and user-friendliness** of the project influence its practicality and accessibility." |
| Relevance | responsive, complete, feasible, usable | now: "fully and directly addresses the challenge … It **meaningfully integrates NASA data**, utilizing it as a core component of the proposed solution rather than a superficial addition." |
| Presentation | communication, story | adds: "Judges evaluate the demo and full project to compute this score." |

Usability now scores under Validity, and NASA-data integration under Relevance. optimisation.md §12a re-maps the
items.

## Hard requirements (eligibility for Global Judging)

- Respond to one official challenge; all members registered for the same Local Event, on the team's Members tab;
  1–6 members (Local Event).
- "Complete all of the required fields on the Project Submission page."
- Demo: "either in the form of a slide presentation (7-slide limit including title and intro slides) or a video
  presentation (30-second limit)". Hosted externally (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, …), "publicly accessible link …
  should not require a password, permission, or registration".
- "Use English language on the project submission page and in any linked materials." A video may be spoken in
  another language but "MUST include English language subtitles". "Slide decks must be in English."
- Click "Submit for Judging" before Sunday 15 Nov, 11:59 p.m. local time of the Local Event. "You may edit and
  resubmit your project as many times as you'd like until the end of the hackathon. Only your last saved
  submission will be reviewed."
- Two boxes to tick: terms and conditions; and "I confirm that the submitted project represents my team's original
  work and that all external resources including code, text and images (even if they are open source or freely
  available) used in the project are listed in the References field".
- "Project demonstrations and final projects may not include any images, video, audio, or other likeness to persons
  under 18 years old."
- "To be eligible for a Global Award, you must use at least one NASA data or resource."

## Super Intelligence (SI) — the 2026 term for AI

- "Participants may use SI tools … Each team's project submission must include a description of how SI tools were
  used during the development of their project."
- Submission form: "SI should not be used to (come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge)."
- "Generated content may not use, contain, or modify any NASA branding or design elements (such as the NASA logos,
  flags, or mission identifiers)."
- "Images and videos: Must include a visible watermark to indicate SI generation. Audio: Must acknowledge SI
  generation in descriptive text and metadata. Code and data: Must acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and
  metadata."
- The SI answer on the form "is for data-collection purposes only, and your response will not be seen by the
  judges. However, judges will be able to see any SI-generated indicators within your project".
  So the acknowledgement must also live in the project itself: README "Use of SI tools", the About page, and the
  project details text.

## Project page fields (Project Submission Guide)

1. Name of the project.
2. High-level summary: "What did you develop? How does it address the challenge? Why is it important?"
3. Demo link (see above). "The majority of judges' evaluation will be based on your project submission."
4. Final project link: "a website you created … or code repository".
5. Project details: "What exactly does it do? How does it work? What benefits does it have? What do you hope to
   achieve? What tools, coding languages, hardware, or software did you use?"
6. NASA data: "Provide specific details about what NASA data and resources you used."
7. Space Agency Partner data (encouraged; partners include ESA, ISRO, CSA, JAXA, UK Space Agency …).
8. References: "List all of the data, resources, and tools used … any code, text, and images (even if they are open
   source or freely available)".
9. SI use (data collection only, not shown to judges).
Images (JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB) can be pasted into the page; videos and documents cannot.

A new website should request a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification (optional, "can help prevent potential website
access issues during judging").
